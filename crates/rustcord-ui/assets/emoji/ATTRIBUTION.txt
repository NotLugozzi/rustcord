Graphics were extracted from the Twemoji Color Emoji SVGinOT font and rasterized
to a single spritesheet (see src/emoji_font_coverage.rs and index.json for the
generation approach); no modifications were made to the artwork itself beyond
cropping to each glyph's natural bounds and scaling to 64x64px.
